Passport of a country with no citizens

Okay, well the uh title may be a biiit of an exaggeration, but its true! The Vatican, or Vatican City has no permanent citizens.

Well, almost. There is one person who has this passport for life, and that's the Pope (Currently Pope Leo XIV)

Other "citizens" hold it only as long as they hold their qualifying role. But this is also a passport with VERY few holders - it's currently held by 650 or so induviduals

Anddd one more thing. You might think that it's a weak passport, mainly issued for formalities, considering the tiny size of the country. But no. This little booklet gets you visa free access to 151 countries- That makes it the ~20th best passport in the world!

The Passport Museum

Passport of... Legoland?

Ah yes, my personal favourite. This is LEGO's official passport! You can obtain this mystical item at participating LEGO stores all around the world! It's a pretty cool collectible. You also get to stamp it at LEGO stores, each store having a custom stamp!

A little fun fact, the first page of this "passport" contains cridentials like your name, address, and date of birth, just like a real passport lol.

Anyways, back to this passport. This is a VERY wierd case - It's not technically a passport, but it also is. It's mainly issued for diplomats who work for the UN

As for how strong this passport is, well, this lies in the grey area. While not very many countries *explicitly* say that this is a recognised travel document, countries that sign the UN charter (the ToS that countries sign before becoming a member of the UN) are technically obligated to recognise this.
